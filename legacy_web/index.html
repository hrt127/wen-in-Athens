Understanding the Greeks:

  • Δ (Delta) — Sensitivity to price changes
  • Γ (Gamma) — Rate of change of Delta
  • Vega — Sensitivity to volatility
  • Θ (Theta) — Time decay (value lost per day)

📖 Glossary

Delta: Steering wheel — how option price moves when underlying moves.
Gamma: Sensitivity of the steering — how quickly Delta changes.
Vega: Volatility compass — sensitivity to implied volatility.
Theta: Time decay clock — how option loses value over time.
